How to play
  • Each player holds three cards. The card turned up under the stock sets the briscola (trump) suit.
  • You don't have to follow suit. The trick goes to the highest trump played; with no trump, to the highest card of the suit that was led.
  • Card order: Ace, 3, King, Knight, Jack, 7, 6, 5, 4, 2.
  • Points: Ace 11, 3 is 10, King 4, Knight 3, Jack 2, others 0. 120 in the deck.
  • The trick winner draws first and leads next. When the stock runs out, play the last three cards.
  • Over 60 points wins. 60–60 is a draw.
